03 / EXPLORATION

Missions

Visit historic landing sites and explore the regions that shaped our understanding of the Red Planet.

Maps are provided by NASA Mars Trek WMTS. Missing coverage means no measurements. Images are archival and weather observations are local. Expeditions route each leg around steep terrain on coarse MOLA topography and estimate daylight, sunlight and air pressure along the way; safety remains unverified. TES thermal inertia is provided as an additional NASA dataset.
